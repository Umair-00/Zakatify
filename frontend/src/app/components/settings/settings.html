The nisab is the minimum wealth threshold before zakat becomes obligatory. Silver (595g) is recommended by AAOIFI and results in a lower threshold, meaning more people qualify to pay zakat. Gold (85g) sets a higher threshold.

Zakat is traditionally calculated on a lunar (Hijri) year at 2.5%. If you use a Gregorian year, the rate adjusts to 2.578% to account for the longer year.

The annual date you assess your wealth and calculate zakat. Many people use the first day of Ramadan or a fixed Gregorian date.
